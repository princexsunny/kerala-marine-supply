Existing frame pattern

A–H members, separate and symmetrical. Curved bottom shape at the bow, V-shaped main bottom and fitted stern frames. No hull plates are generated.

Default angle: 2 × 2 in × 6 mm = 50.8 × 50.8 × 6 mm. Change the frame member section below.

Frame member section

Section choices apply to all A–H members. Flat depth lies in the frame plane. Round sections use diameter; width is ignored. Solid rod ignores wall thickness. Regenerate after changing size. These remain envelope-fitted guides; section orientation and joints require CAD detailing.

Stations & connections

The original first frame is 2 ft aft of the main-hull forward datum. At 65 ft, 2 ft spacing gives 32 frames at stations 2–64 ft.

Bottom-angle upper ends seat at the two 16 mm keel side faces. Main connection inset defaults to 20 mm; the stern uses the approved 8 mm allowance. Chine seats and top-angle ends are marked for coping.

Frame drawing

Deck and lower crossbars retain the existing pattern. They lift locally only where needed to clear a rising bottom. Each member receives its own part ID.
